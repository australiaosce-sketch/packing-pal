## 4. Brand story & market opportunity

**4.1 Elevator pitch**
Skincare by Dr V is a doctor-formulated skincare, sun care and hair care brand made specifically for skin of colour: Black, South Asian, Middle Eastern and mixed-heritage skin. Every formula is developed in our own lab with melanin-rich skin as the starting point and clinically tested on skin of colour. Our promise: even, glowing skin without the irritation and dark marks that products made for Caucasian skin can leave behind.

<sub>430/500 characters</sub>

**4.2 Consumer insight / problem solved**
Melanin-rich skin reacts to irritation by making more pigment. Strong acids, harsh retinoids and some lasers that suit Caucasian skin can leave her with new dark marks. Most products are formulated and clinically tested on Caucasian skin first. Our customer tells us the same story: she has tried everything, from drugstore to luxury, and reacted or seen no change. She needs results without the risk.

<sub>401/500 characters</sub>

**4.3 Unique value proposition**
Built for skin of colour from the first ingredient, not adapted for it. Formulated by a medical doctor in a lab dedicated to skin of colour, and clinically tested on it. Results come from stacking several gentle actives rather than one harsh one. We treat concerns that matter most to her (pigmentation, dark marks, melasma, dark circles, ingrown hairs, thinning hair) as complete systems, not single products.

<sub>410/500 characters</sub>

**4.4 Target consumer**
Women of colour aged 25 to 55 (Black, South Asian, Middle Eastern, mixed heritage) who are confident, well informed and willing to invest in their skin. She has been an afterthought for most premium brands. She researches ingredients, watches long-form education and buys whole routines once she trusts a brand. Secondary: Black men with razor bumps, and men and women 35+ with thinning hair.

<sub>392/500 characters</sub>

**4.5 Why Sephora?**
Our customer already shops at Sephora. Before she finds us, she tries the bestsellers there, but few were made with her skin as the starting point. Sephora is where she discovers prestige beauty, and where a doctor-led brand built for her belongs. We bring a loyal, highly engaged community, a proven UK base (46% of our sales) and a range that fills a clear gap. Sephora gives us the European shelf and the in-store expertise to reach her.

<sub>440/500 characters</sub>
