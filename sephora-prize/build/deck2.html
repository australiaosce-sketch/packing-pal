Market opportunity & white space

Skin of colour has been studied last. We study it first.

The problem. Melanin-rich skin answers irritation by making more pigment. The strong acids, harsh retinoids and some lasers that suit Caucasian skin can leave her with new dark marks. Most formulas are developed and trialled on Caucasian skin first.

The result. She has spent years and money on products that weren't made for her. She tells us so every day:

“I've tried everything and nothing has worked.”
“I bought every vitamin C and it burnt my skin.”
“From the cheapest to the most expensive, I'm always disappointed.”

Target consumer profile

She's informed, she invests, and she's been an afterthought.

Core consumer

Women of colour, 25 to 55

Black, South Asian, Middle Eastern and mixed heritage. Successful, confident and well informed. She reads ingredient lists, watches long-form education and buys whole routines once she trusts a brand.

Her concerns: dark marks that stay for months, uneven tone, melasma, and irritation from products that promised brightening.

Her fear: that anything strong enough to work will leave her skin worse.

Teens and young adults with acne

Who get all three blemishes: acne, red marks and brown marks.

Black men with razor bumps

A large, underserved need. Exfoliate and calm, instead of picking and scarring.

Men and women 35+ with thinning hair

Especially around menopause or after stress.
